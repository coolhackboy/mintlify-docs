# Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you ship.

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="What is Vidgo API?">
Vidgo API is an AI infrastructure platform. It gives you unified access to top AI models with one key, predictable pricing, and production-ready reliability.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I get an API key?">
Create an account and generate your API key instantly from the dashboard.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a free tier?">
New accounts receive free credits so you can test models before scaling.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which models are available?">
We support leading image, video, and music models, with new additions every week.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do you offer enterprise support?">
Yes. Contact our team to discuss custom pricing, SLAs, and dedicated support.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Where can I check current rates?">
See the [Pricing page](https://vidgo.ai/apis/api-pricing) or your Vidgo dashboard for current model rates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What affects generation costs?">
Cost depends on the selected model and its supported billing options. Image generation can depend on quality, resolution, output count, and reference image count. Video generation can depend on duration, resolution, audio generation, and reference-video duration. See each model page for the factors that apply to that workflow.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Which music model do you use?">
We use Suno's official models, including the latest v5 model. Our music API supports generating tracks up to 8 minutes long with professional quality.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Will I be charged for failed requests?">
No. We have transparent pricing and only charge for successful generations. Failed requests will automatically refund your credits.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What payment methods do you support?">
We support multiple payment methods including Stripe (credit/debit cards), WeChat Pay, and cryptocurrency payments for maximum flexibility.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How can I contact support if I have issues?">
You can reach us at support@vidgo.ai. We're also gradually launching additional support channels like Discord - stay tuned for announcements on our website.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
