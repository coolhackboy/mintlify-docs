All Chat Series endpoints use `https://api.vidgo.ai` and a Vidgo API key. Send the key as `Authorization: Bearer VIDGO_API_KEY`. Claude clients may also use `x-api-key`; Gemini clients may use `x-goog-api-key`.

## Model protocol support

| Model | Responses | Chat Completions | Claude Messages | Gemini native |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| `gpt-5-6-luna` | Yes | — | — | — |
| `gpt-5-6-terra` | Yes | — | — | — |
| `gpt-5-6-sol` | Yes | — | — | — |
| `kimi-k3` | — | Yes | — | — |
| `claude-sonnet-5` | — | Yes | Yes | — |
| `claude-opus-5` | — | Yes | Yes | — |

The legacy IDs above keep their existing protocol support. Gemini workflows are listed below.

Both normal JSON responses and SSE streaming are supported.
