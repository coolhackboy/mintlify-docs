Turn written scene concepts into short videos with Gemini Omni Flash, with landscape or portrait framing and output up to 4K.

## Models

- **google/gemini-omni-flash/text-to-video** — Generate video scenes from text prompts.

## Features

- Generate video scenes from text prompts.
- Choose from `720p`, `1080p`, `4k` output resolutions.
- Supports landscape (`16:9`) and portrait (`9:16`) framing.

## Required parameters

- `prompt` (string): Describe the scene and desired motion. The prompt must not be blank when supplied. Maximum length: **20,000 characters**.

## Optional parameters

- `resolution` (string): `720p`, `1080p`, `4k`. Default: `720p`.
- `duration` (integer): 4, 6, 8, 10 seconds. Default: 6 seconds.
- `aspect_ratio` (string): `16:9`, `9:16`. Default: `16:9`.

## Notes

Send only the input fields supported by this workflow. Unsupported `input` fields are rejected before task creation.
