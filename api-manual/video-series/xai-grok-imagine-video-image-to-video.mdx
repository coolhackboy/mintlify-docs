Animate a still image with Grok Imagine, using text to shape the scene in six- or ten-second video clips.

## Models

- **xai/grok-imagine-video/image-to-video** — Generate video from image input.

## Features

- Generate video from image input.
- Accepts exactly 1 image in `image_urls`.

## Required parameters

- `prompt` (string): Describe the desired scene and motion. Enter 1–5,000 characters.
- `image_urls` (array of strings): Provide exactly one publicly accessible HTTP(S) image URL.

## Optional parameters

- `mode` (string): Generation style. Choose fun, normal, or spicy. Values: `fun`, `normal`, `spicy`.
- `duration` (integer): Video duration in seconds. Choose 6 or 10; defaults to 6. Values: `6`, `10`.

## Notes

Supply publicly accessible HTTP(S) URLs for reference images.
