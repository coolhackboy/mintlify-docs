Generate high-quality videos using Grok Imagine. Create engaging content with text-to-video and image-to-video capabilities.

## Models

- **grok-imagine** — Video generation (text-to-video and image-to-video)

## Features

- **Flexible Modes**: Text-to-video and image-to-video
- **Generation Styles**: Choose from "fun", "normal", or "spicy" modes
- **Aspect Ratios**: 1:1 (square), 2:3 (portrait), and 3:2 (landscape)
- **Video Duration**: `6` or `10` seconds

## Input modes

- **Text to video:** omit `image_urls`.
- **Image to video:** supply `image_urls` to animate an input image.

## Required parameters

- `prompt` (string): Generation prompt describing the desired video motion. Maximum length: 5,000 characters.

## Optional parameters

- `image_urls` (string[]): Reference image URLs for image-to-video generation (required for image-to-video, max 1 image). Maximum items: 1.
- `aspect_ratio` (string): Video aspect ratio (text-to-video only). Accepted values: `1:1`, `2:3`, `3:2`.
- `duration` (integer): The duration of the generated video in seconds. Accepted values: `6`, `10`.
- `mode` (string): Generation style mode. Accepted values: `fun`, `normal`, `spicy`.
