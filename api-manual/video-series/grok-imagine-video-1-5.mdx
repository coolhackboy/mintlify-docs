Create 1–15 second videos from text, a single first-frame image, or up to seven reference images.

## Models

- **grok-imagine-video-1.5** — Grok Imagine Video 1.5 video generation

## Input modes

- **Text to video:** omit both image fields. Supports `480p`, `720p`, and `1080p`.
- **Image to video:** provide exactly one URL in `image_urls`. Supports `480p`, `720p`, and `1080p`.
- **Reference to video:** provide 1–7 URLs in `reference_image_urls` and refer to them as `<IMAGE_0>`, `<IMAGE_1>`, and so on. Supports `480p` and `720p`.

`image_urls` and `reference_image_urls` cannot be used together. `aspect_ratio` is available for text and reference modes, but not first-frame image-to-video.

## Required parameters

- `prompt` (string): Up to 4,096 characters.

## Optional parameters

- `image_urls` (string[]): Provide publicly accessible image URLs. Minimum items: 1. Maximum items: 1.
- `reference_image_urls` (string[]): Provide reference image URLs. Minimum items: 1. Maximum items: 7.
- `resolution` (string): `480p`, `720p`, or `1080p`. Default: `720p`.
- `duration` (integer): Integer from `1` to `15`. Default: `6` for text/image mode and `8` for reference mode.
- `aspect_ratio` (string): `16:9`, `4:3`, `3:2`, `1:1`, `2:3`, `3:4`, or `9:16`. Default: `16:9` where supported.
