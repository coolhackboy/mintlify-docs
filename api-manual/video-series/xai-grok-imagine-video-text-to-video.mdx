Create six- or ten-second video scenes with Grok Imagine, using text to guide the action in square, portrait, or landscape formats.

## Models

- **xai/grok-imagine-video/text-to-video** — Generate video scenes from text prompts.

## Features

- Generate video scenes from text prompts.

## Required parameters

- `prompt` (string): Describe the desired scene and motion. Enter 1–5,000 characters.

## Optional parameters

- `aspect_ratio` (string): Optional video aspect ratio: 1:1, 2:3, 3:2, 16:9, or 9:16. Values: `1:1`, `2:3`, `3:2`, `16:9`, `9:16`.
- `mode` (string): Generation style. Choose fun, normal, or spicy. Values: `fun`, `normal`, `spicy`.
- `duration` (integer): Video duration in seconds. Choose 6 or 10; defaults to 6. Values: `6`, `10`.
