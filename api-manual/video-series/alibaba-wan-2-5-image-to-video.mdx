Animate one reference image into a 5 or 10 second video with synchronized audio and output up to 1080p.

## Models

- `alibaba/wan-2.5/image-to-video` — Animate a reference image using a motion prompt.

## Required parameters

- `prompt` (string): Describe the subject, scene, and motion. Use 1–1500 characters after trimming surrounding whitespace.
- `image_urls` (string[]): Provide exactly one publicly accessible HTTP(S) image URL.

## Optional parameters

- `resolution` (string): Output resolution: 480p, 720p, or 1080p. Default: 720p.
- `duration` (integer): Video duration: 5 or 10 seconds. Default: 5.
- `audio` (string): Audio file URL to supply a recording for the video.
- `negative_prompt` (string): Description of elements to exclude from the scene.
- `seed` (integer): Random seed for reusing generation settings.

## Notes

### Retrieve the Video

Query the task with `task_id`. Continue polling while `status` is `not_started` or `running`. At `finished`, read the video URL from `data.files[].file_url`. At `failed`, read `data.error_message` for the error details.
