Create a video from image, video and audio references.

## Models

- **minimax/h3-max/reference-to-video** — Create a video from image, video and audio references.

## Required parameters

- `prompt` (string): Non-empty prompt describing the target video. Must contain at least one non-whitespace character. Length: 1–50,000 characters.
- `reference_image_urls` (array of strings): Required reference images: static JPEG/PNG/WebP, at most 30 MB each. Supported shapes: 1:1, 4:3, 3:4, 16:9, 9:16, 5:2, 2:5. Normalize orientation before uploading. 1–9 items.

## Optional parameters

- `duration` (integer): Requested output duration in seconds. Range: 5–15. Default: `5`.
- `resolution` (string): Output resolution. Values: `480p`, `768p`, `1080p`. Default: `768p`.
- `enable_safety_checker` (boolean): Set false to disable the safety checker. Default: `true`.
- `aspect_ratio` (string): Output aspect ratio. Values: `21:9`, `16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, `3:4`, `9:16`, `adaptive`. Default: `adaptive`.
- `reference_video_urls` (array of strings): MP4/MOV, at most 50 MB each, 2-15 seconds per clip and at most 15 seconds combined. Constant 24 fps, square pixels, zero rotation, 16:9 or 9:16. After capping at output duration, counted frames must be 48, 120, 240 or 360. At most 3 items.
- `reference_audio_urls` (array of strings): MP3/WAV, at most 15 MB each, 2-15 seconds per clip and at most 15 seconds combined. Mono/stereo, 32 kHz, decoded sample count divisible by 800. Each clip must not exceed output duration. At most 3 items.

## Notes

- Provide at least one reference image. Starting and ending frames cannot be combined with reference assets.
- Total reference assets across image, video, and audio lists must not exceed 12.
- Refer to assets as `Image 1`, `Video 1` and `Audio 1` in list order.
- All reference URLs must be public HTTP(S) download links.
- Use an integer for `duration`; fractional durations are rejected.
