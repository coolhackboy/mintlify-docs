## Usage Guide

Use `minimax/h3-max/reference-to-video`. When this workflow becomes available, provide at least one reference image. Optionally add reference video and audio, with at most 12 assets across all lists. Refer to assets as `Image 1`, `Video 1` and `Audio 1` in list order. Starting and ending frames cannot be combined with reference assets.

Set `model` and optional `callback_url` at the request root. Place generation parameters inside `input`.

## Parameter Details

- `prompt`: required non-empty string, up to 50,000 characters.
- `duration`: integer from 5 through 15 seconds; defaults to 5. Fractional durations are rejected.
- `resolution`: `480p`, `768p` or `1080p`; defaults to `768p`.
- `aspect_ratio`: six fixed ratios (`21:9`, `16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, `3:4`, `9:16`) or `adaptive`; defaults to `adaptive`.
- `reference_image_urls`: required array of 1-9 static JPEG, PNG or WebP images, at most 30 MB each. Supported shapes are `1:1`, `4:3`, `3:4`, `16:9`, `9:16`, `5:2` and `2:5`; normalize image orientation before uploading.
- `reference_video_urls`: optional array of up to 3 MP4/MOV clips, at most 50 MB each. Each clip must be 2-15 seconds, with at most 15 seconds combined. Clips must use constant 24 fps, square pixels, zero rotation metadata, and a `16:9` or `9:16` shape. After limiting frames to the requested output duration, the counted frames must be 48, 120, 240 or 360. Other timing patterns are rejected.
- `reference_audio_urls`: optional array of up to 3 MP3/WAV clips, at most 15 MB each. Each clip must be 2-15 seconds and no longer than the output duration, with at most 15 seconds combined. Audio must be mono or stereo at 32 kHz, with a decoded sample count divisible by 800.
- All reference URLs must be public HTTP(S) download links. Total assets across the three lists must not exceed 12.

## Optional parameters

- `enable_safety_checker`: boolean, defaults to `true`. Set `false` to disable it.
- `callback_url`: optional webhook URL at the request root. Query task status if you do not use a callback.
