Animate a single image using a prompt that describes subject motion, camera movement, and sound. The image supplies the first frame and determines the video’s aspect ratio.

## Models

- **xai/grok-imagine-video-v1.5/image-to-video** — Image-to-video generation with first-frame guidance

## Required parameters

- `prompt` (string): Describe subject motion, camera movement, and sound in 1–4096 characters.
- `image_urls` (array of strings): Provide exactly one publicly accessible HTTP(S) image URL as the first frame.

## Optional parameters

- `resolution` (string): Output resolution. Default: `720p`. Values: `480p`, `720p`, `1080p`.
- `duration` (integer): Integer video duration from 1 to 15 seconds. Default: `6`.

## Notes

- The prompt must contain at least one non-whitespace character.
- Use a publicly accessible HTTP(S) image URL with a hostname and no embedded credentials.
