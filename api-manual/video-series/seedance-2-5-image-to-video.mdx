## Available Models

- **seedance-2.5/image-to-video** - Seedance 2.5 image to video generation

## Input modes

- This model uses one starting frame and an optional ending frame through `image_urls`.
- `image_urls` is required and cannot be combined with reference-media fields.

## Important notes

- `prompt`, `duration`, and `resolution` are required.
- `duration` accepts integer values from 4 to 30 seconds.
- `resolution` accepts `480p` or `720p`.
- `aspect_ratio` accepts `auto`, `21:9`, `16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, `3:4`, or `9:16`.
- `image_urls` accepts one starting frame and one optional ending frame.
- All media URLs must be publicly accessible and directly downloadable.
- Usage is billed by output duration and selected resolution.
